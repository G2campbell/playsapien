About

A small set of daily puzzle games. One puzzle a day per game, the same one for everybody, rolling over at midnight UTC.

The games

Sojourner is a game of geography. Five places a day, an unlabelled globe, and a story for every place you find — each one checked against a museum, a university or a library, and linked so you can read the original.

Word Chain is a word puzzle. Eight words, every neighbouring pair a compound, first letters only, and the clock is the score.

How it works

Everything is one page in your browser. There is no app to install, nothing to download, and no account needed to play — open a game and it starts. Your scores and settings live in your own browser until you decide otherwise.

An account is optional and does three things a single browser cannot: it keeps your streak when you change phone, it syncs your laptop and your phone, and it lets you compare a day with a friend. Signing in is a six-digit code by email, or Google if you prefer. There are no passwords on this site, which is the most reliable way of never leaking one.

The day rolls over at midnight UTC, the same instant for every player everywhere, so nobody's "today" is anybody else's yesterday.

What this site does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising network.
  • No analytics, no tag manager, no session recording, no tracking pixels.
  • No leaderboard you did not choose. Boards are opt-out, they never show your email address, and you can keep yours to friends only or stay off them entirely — the privacy page has the detail.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. There is one email address per account and it never goes anywhere.
  • No consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to.

Word Chain takes submissions

If you make a chain you are pleased with, you can submit it. Accepted chains are published as daily or practice puzzles, credited to you if you want the credit. What happens to a submission is set out in the terms.
