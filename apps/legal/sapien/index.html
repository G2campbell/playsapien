What Sapien includes

  • Past daily puzzles. Play any of the last 30 days’ daily puzzles you missed, in every game. (They count for your own record, not that day’s rankings.)

  • Friends, your way. Create friend groups and compare against just your friends, as well as against everyone.

  • Make and share puzzles. Build puzzles of your own — starting with Word Chain chains — and send them to friends or submit them for everyone.
