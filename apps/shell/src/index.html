PlaySapien

About

A small workshop of daily puzzles. One a day, each one a different kind of thinking — a globe, a chain of words, and whatever is next on the bench.

The mark is two rings crossed by two rules — a knot you cannot pull apart by force, which is the idea the name carries. It is set the way it was first proofed: pressed into a sheet of cold-press rag paper.

Every game keeps your progress on your own device. An account is optional, and only exists so a streak survives a new phone and so a friend can see how you did today.
